How we teach

What a session looks like

Dual-control cars

The instructor has a second brake and clutch. Nothing you do in the first week can go badly wrong.

Simulator first

Clutch control, gear changes and hazard response practised before you are in live traffic.

Real roads, real conditions

Market streets, the bypass, night driving and rain — the situations that actually make people nervous.

Licence paperwork handled
